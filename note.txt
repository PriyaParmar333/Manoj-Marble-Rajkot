Manoj Marble Visualizer — Project Notes
🎯 Project Goal

Manoj Marble ke liye ek professional website banana jisme apartment/building ke customers ko unke actual room/photo par selected marble ka realistic preview dikhaya ja sake.

Main purpose:

Customer ko marble physically imagine karne ki problem solve karna + Manoj Marble ke liye professional marketing aur lead generation karna.

💡 Main Idea

Jab hum kisi bade apartment/project me marketing ke liye jayenge:

Customer ke room/apartment ki photo lenge.
Website me photo upload karenge.
Manoj Marble ka particular marble select karenge.
AI us marble ko room ke relevant surface par visualize karega.
Customer ko Before → AI Preview dikhayenge.
Customer interested ho to quotation/enquiry bhej sakta hai.
Enquiry WhatsApp par Manoj Marble ko bheji ja sakti hai.
Example
Customer's Actual Room Photo
            ↓
     Upload to Website
            ↓
     Select Marble
            ↓
       AI Visualizer
            ↓
  Realistic Marble Preview
            ↓
     Request Quotation
            ↓
         WhatsApp

🧠 Why We Are Building This

Traditional marble marketing:

Shop → Catalogue → Explain marble → Customer imagines result

Our approach:

Customer's actual apartment
          ↓
       Our marble
          ↓
    Visual preview
          ↓
Customer sees the result

The second approach should make the sales conversation more concrete.
